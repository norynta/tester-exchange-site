Rejected with "more testing required"? Here is how to fix it

Last updated: November 24, 2026

The fix is almost always the same: keep the closed test running, show real daily use by at least 12 opted-in testers, and rewrite your answers with numbers. Why Google says "more testing required"

Google's Help Center says you may need to continue your closed test before you can get production access, and lists causes such as fewer than 12 opted-in testers or testers who were not engaged enough with the app (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465). Google does not publish the exact check it runs.

  • Testers installed once and never came back. The most common reason people report.
  • Dropouts took you under 12. Count again on the day you applied, not on day one.
  • Thin answers. One or two general sentences per question, with no numbers or changes you made.

The 2-week fix plan

  1. Keep your closed track and active testers. Do not delete the track or start a new app.
  2. Recruit up to 15 or more testers so a dropout cannot take you under 12.
  3. Ask every tester to open the app on their own phone every day for 14 days. Ask for one short written note per week.
  4. Upload at least one new build during the run and write down what changed and why.
  5. On day 10, collect your numbers: testers opted in, tester-days of real use, feedback received, bugs fixed, builds shipped.
  6. Apply on day 15 or later, with answers built from those numbers.

Sample answers to adapt

These examples use made-up numbers to show the level of detail Google's questions need. Replace every number and detail with your own, and never claim anything that did not happen.

How easy was it to recruit testers? Pick the option that is honestly true. Google asks you to choose, not to write text.

Describe the engagement you received from testers. Example: "15 testers were opted in on the day we applied. 13 opened the app on at least 10 of the 14 days. Most sessions were 2 to 5 minutes. Testers used the main flow (sign up, create an item, share it) and the settings screen."

Summarize the feedback you received. Example: "We received 21 written messages. The top three issues were a crash when rotating the phone, a confusing second onboarding screen, and slow loading on mobile data."

What changes did you make based on testing? Example: "We fixed the rotation crash in build 1.0.2, cut onboarding from 4 screens to 2 in build 1.0.3, and cached the first list so it loads in about 1 second."

How do you know the app is ready for production? Example: "No crashes were reported in the last 7 days, all high-priority issues are closed, and 13 of 15 testers kept using the app until the end of the test."

Frequently asked questions

Does "more testing required" mean my app is banned?

No. Google says you may need to keep running your closed test. Keep your track and active testers, improve the evidence, and apply again.

Do I have to restart the 14 days from zero?

Google does not say you must start from zero, but you should plan for at least another 14 days of real daily use with 12 or more opted-in testers before reapplying.

How long should I wait before applying again?

Google does not publish a fixed waiting period. Let the evidence build for about two weeks, then apply when you have numbers to show.

Will a tester service guarantee approval?

No service can guarantee approval, because Google makes the decision. A good service gives you real daily use and records you can use in your answers.

Can I copy the sample answers?

Only as a format. Use your own real numbers and facts. Answers that do not match your test data can hurt your application.
