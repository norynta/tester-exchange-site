What you buy

Credits and tester packages on Tester Exchange are digital services. They are used to get your app tested by real people on Google Play's closed testing track. Credits have no cash value and do not convert back to money.

No refunds

All purchases are final. We do not offer refunds, because the testing work starts as soon as your order is paid. If something we deliver does not work as described, email support@norynta.com and we will fix it or deliver the missing testing.

Payments and taxes

Prices are shown in US dollars unless your checkout shows your local currency. Payments are processed by Dodo Payments (merchant of record, cards and local payment methods), PayPal, or crypto. Sales tax or VAT is calculated and collected at checkout where required, and the total you pay is shown before you confirm.

Fair use

Do not list apps that break Google Play policy, and do not abuse the testing exchange. We may remove listings or accounts that do, and credits on such accounts may be forfeited.
